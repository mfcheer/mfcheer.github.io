时栖支持

TimeNook · 横屏时钟与日程

感谢使用时栖。遇到问题、想提出建议或需要隐私帮助，请发送邮件至 469236905@qq.com。

日程没有显示

  1. 在 iPhone「设置」>「隐私与安全性」>「日历」中确认已允许时栖访问日历。
  2. 在时栖的「设置」>「日历来源」中确认需要显示的日历已开启。
  3. 回到主界面下拉刷新，或重启应用后重试。

如何进入纯时钟模式

双击主时钟可隐藏日程面板；点击右上角按钮即可恢复日程视图。

如何减少动画或耗电

应用会在系统开启「减少动态效果」、低电量模式或设备过热时自动降低动画或亮度，并通过细微像素位移保护长期显示。正常电量下，亮度由你通过主界面手势自行调节。你也可在应用设置中关闭「桌面宠物」。

提交问题时请附上

请提供 iPhone 型号、iOS 版本、应用版本、问题发生步骤及截图（如方便）。请不要通过邮件发送完整日历内容或其他敏感信息。

TimeNook Support

Landscape clock and calendar

For help, feedback, or privacy requests, email 469236905@qq.com.

Events are not appearing

  1. In iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Calendars, confirm that TimeNook has calendar access.
  2. In TimeNook, open Settings > Calendar Sources and enable the calendars you want to show.
  3. Return to the clock and refresh, or relaunch the app.

Clock-only mode

Double-tap the main clock to hide the schedule panel. Use the button at the top right to restore it.

Reduce animation or power use

TimeNook automatically reduces animation or brightness during Low Power Mode, Reduce Motion, or elevated thermal state, and uses subtle pixel shifting for long displays. At normal power, brightness remains under your manual control. You can also turn off Desktop Pet in the app's settings.

When reporting an issue

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, steps to reproduce, and a screenshot if possible. Do not email complete calendar content or other sensitive information.
