隐私政策

时栖 TimeNook · 生效日期：2026 年 8 月 24 日

时栖是一款在横屏中显示时间与日程的本地工具。我们重视你的隐私，并尽量只使用实现核心功能所必需的信息。

我们如何使用日历

当你授权日历访问权限后，时栖仅在你的设备上读取你选择的日历及近期日程，用于显示日程标题、时间和所属日历。时栖不会修改、创建或删除你的日历内容。

我们不会收集或共享的数据

当前版本不提供账号系统、广告、分析追踪或云端同步。日历内容、你选择的日历来源、显示偏好和桌面宠物状态仅保存在你的设备上，不会上传、出售或分享给第三方。

本地设置与设备信息

时栖会在本机保存必要的显示设置、语言选择、日历筛选和宠物状态，以便下次打开时恢复你的体验。应用也会根据系统的低电量模式、减少动态效果和设备热状态降低动画或亮度，并微移主界面内容，以减少干扰、耗电和长期静态显示风险。这些信息不离开你的设备。

你的选择

你可随时在 iOS「设置」中关闭时栖的日历访问权限；关闭后，应用将无法显示系统日历日程。你也可在应用内更改日历来源、语言和桌面宠物设置。

儿童隐私

时栖不面向儿童收集任何个人信息，也不包含广告或行为追踪。

政策更新与联系我们

如果你主动通过邮件提交反馈，我们会收到你在邮件中提供的内容及邮件地址，并仅用于回复和处理问题。请勿发送完整日历内容或其他敏感信息。

若本政策发生实质变更，我们会更新本页的生效日期。对隐私问题、数据请求或删除本地设置的帮助，请联系：469236905@qq.com。

Privacy Policy

TimeNook · Effective August 24, 2026

TimeNook is a local, landscape clock and calendar utility. We use only the information needed to provide its core experience.

Calendar access

If you grant calendar permission, TimeNook reads the calendars and upcoming events you choose on your device to display event titles, times, and calendar names. It never creates, edits, or deletes calendar events.

Data we do not collect or share

This version has no accounts, ads, analytics tracking, or cloud synchronization. Calendar content, selected calendars, display preferences, and pet state remain on your device and are not uploaded, sold, or shared with third parties.

Local settings and device state

The app stores display settings, language selection, calendar filters, and pet state locally so it can restore your experience. It may use Low Power Mode, Reduce Motion, and thermal state to reduce animation or brightness, and it subtly shifts the main display to reduce prolonged static-image risk. This information stays on your device.

Your choices

You can revoke calendar access in iOS Settings at any time. Without access, TimeNook cannot show your system calendar events. You can also change calendar sources, language, and Desktop Pet settings in the app.

Contact and updates

If you voluntarily email feedback, we receive the message content and your email address and use them only to respond and resolve the issue. Do not send complete calendar content or other sensitive information.

We will update this page's effective date if this policy materially changes. For privacy questions or help deleting local settings, contact 469236905@qq.com.
